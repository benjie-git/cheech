Cheech Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Cheech does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. There is nothing to manage, export, or delete, because
nothing about you ever leaves your devices.

What we do not do

- We do not collect or track any personal data.
- We do not include analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- We do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email, or
  contacts.
- We do not operate any servers. Games are played directly between the
  devices of the people playing.

What stays on your device

- Your player name, color, seat layout, and other game preferences are
  saved locally on your device so your next game is ready to go.
- Hostnames of games you have joined are saved locally to make joining
  again easier.

Multiplayer connections

When you play a multiplayer game, your device connects directly to the
host device (or accepts direct connections from players you invite) over
your local network. Game data -- board moves, player names, and chat
messages -- is exchanged only between the devices of the players in that
game. The developer never sees any of it.

Invite links

Invite links you share contain the address of the hosting device, so
treat them like you would any invitation to your home network: only
share them with people you want to play with.

Children

Cheech collects no data from anyone, including children.
